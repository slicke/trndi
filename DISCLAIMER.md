## Not a medical device

Trndi is a hobby project that displays data from other systems. It is not
CE-marked, not FDA-cleared, and not registered as a medical device in any
jurisdiction.

It is NOT intended for:

- Medical diagnosis, treatment, or prevention
- Making medical decisions
- Replacing your CGM app or medical devices
- Emergency medical situations

Always verify readings with your official CGM device or app, and consult a
healthcare professional for medical concerns or emergencies. Never make a
medical decision based solely on this app.

## Alerts and notifications are not an alarm system

Trndi can show high and low alerts, system notifications, spoken warnings, and
tray or taskbar badges. **Do not rely on them.** An alert may be delayed, or may
never reach you at all — for example when:

- Trndi is closed, suspended, or the machine is asleep
- The operating system is in Do Not Disturb / Focus mode, or has denied,
  silenced, or coalesced Trndi's notifications
- Alerts are snoozed, or were snoozed and not re-enabled
- No new reading arrives, because the sensor, the network, or the backend
  service is unavailable
- Sound is muted, the display is off, or the app is on a screen you are not
  looking at

A missing alert never means your glucose is in range. Use a device intended and
approved for alarming.

## Data comes from third-party services

Trndi reads from Nightscout, Dexcom, Tandem Source, CareLink, xDrip, and similar
services. Accuracy, timeliness, and availability of that data are outside
Trndi's control. Values shown may be inaccurate, stale, incomplete, or absent,
and Trndi may display them without being able to detect that they are wrong.
